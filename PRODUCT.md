# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A developer who reads many books in PDF format, mostly during long focused sessions on a Mac.
They live in keyboard-driven tools (editors, terminals, Raycast) and expect to drive everything without touching the mouse.

## Product Purpose

A local, private PDF reading app for books.
It keeps a persistent library, remembers exactly where you were in every book, and makes reading, navigating, searching, and annotating fast and calm.
Success: a book opens where you left it in under a second, every action has a shortcut, and nothing on screen competes with the text.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, invisible.
The tool disappears into the book, like Notion disappears into the document.
No branding on screen, no chrome that lingers, no decoration without function.

## Anti-references

- Adobe Acrobat: toolbar walls, panels everywhere, upsells.
- Browser default PDF viewers: gray industrial chrome, no memory, no library.
- "Feature-rich" readers that expose every setting as a visible button.

## Design Principles

- Keyboard first, mouse optional: every feature reachable by shortcut and by Cmd+K palette; shortcuts are discoverable, never memorization homework.
- Chrome earns its place: UI appears when summoned, gets out of the way while reading.
- Familiarity over novelty: standard affordances done extremely well (palette, sidebars, kbd hints), no invented controls.
- Reading comfort is the metric: page rendering quality, themes for ambient light, and restored positions beat any feature count.
- Everything persists: position, zoom, highlights, bookmarks, theme - closing the app costs nothing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Full keyboard operability (also an accessibility feature), visible focus rings.
- Text contrast at or above WCAG AA in all three themes.
- Reduced-motion users get instant transitions (prefers-reduced-motion honored).
